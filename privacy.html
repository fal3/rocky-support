Rocky Privacy Policy

Effective July 29, 2026

Rocky does not create its own user accounts, transmit its local gameplay analytics to the developer, or track users. Anonymous aggregate gameplay counts are stored locally. Game Center is optional and is not required for any gameplay.

Information stored on the device

Rocky stores game progress, best scores, stars, and audio, graphics, haptics, and accessibility preferences locally. This information is used only to provide the game and restore the player's choices.

Rocky keeps a bounded aggregate summary of app opens, run selections, starts, outcomes, and totals or fixed buckets for statistics such as score, duration, melts, and combos. It stores no raw analytics event history, account identifier, device identifier, or installation identifier. Rocky also retains up to ten completed-run replay journals, including the level rules, random seed, inputs, and final checkpoint, for deterministic replay verification. Rocky does not transmit this data or provide it to the developer or third parties. The operating system may include local app data in backups according to the player's backup settings.

Rocky uses system preferences to save settings and the system's monotonic clock to measure elapsed gameplay time. If a player signs into Apple Game Center, Rocky submits eligible scores and achievements to Apple. Signed-out results are never uploaded later.

Sharing and third parties

Rocky does not sell player information. Optional Game Center player profiles, scores, and achievements are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until the player chooses Reset progress in Settings or deletes Rocky. Reset progress deletes level completion, stars, scores, and the local aggregate gameplay summary. The rolling replay journals remain in Rocky's app data until newer runs replace them or the player removes the data. On iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro, deleting Rocky removes its app-container data. On Mac, support files may remain in ~/Library/Containers/fallah.Rocky/Data/Library/Application Support/Rocky until the player removes that folder. Game Center records are managed through the player's Apple Account.

Children

Rocky does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other player.

Changes and contact

If Rocky's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the change ships. Questions can be sent to Rocky Support.
