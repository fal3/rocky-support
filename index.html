Rocky: Falling Sand Puzzle

Stack. Melt. Chain combos.

Download free on the App Store

iPhone · iPad · Mac · Apple Vision Pro

Rocky is an original falling-sand puzzle game. Colored pieces drop into a deep well and dissolve into thousands of flowing grains. Build a connected path of one color from wall to wall to melt it, clear space, and launch cascading combos.

Features

  • Flowing, pooling sand driven by a deterministic simulation
  • Wall-to-wall melts, cascades, combos, and score multipliers
  • 20 handcrafted missions across four mountain chapters, plus endless mode
  • Crisp 2D and glossy Dimensional presentations over one simulation
  • Colorblind palettes, grain patterns, placement hints, and Reduce Motion support
  • Optional Game Center leaderboards and achievements
  • Fully playable offline

Support

Quick help

  • Open Settings in Rocky to adjust audio, haptics, graphics, controls, and accessibility options.
  • If gameplay appears paused, open the pause menu and choose Resume.
  • To start the campaign over, use Reset progress in Settings.
  • If the app stops responding, close it, reopen it, and install the latest available update.

Report a problem

Email Rocky Support or open a GitHub issue. Include your device, operating-system version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Privacy

Rocky has no Rocky accounts, advertising, or tracking. Its bounded gameplay summary stays on the device and is never sent to the developer. Optional Game Center features are provided by Apple. Read the Rocky Privacy Policy.
