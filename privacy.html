Rocky Privacy Policy

Effective July 14, 2026

Rocky does not create user accounts, show advertising, use analytics, track users, or send personal information off the device.

Information stored on the device

Rocky stores game progress, best scores, stars, and audio, graphics, haptics, and accessibility preferences locally. This information is used only to provide the game and restore the player's choices.

Rocky uses system preferences to save settings and the system's monotonic clock to measure elapsed gameplay time. These values remain on the device and are never used for fingerprinting or tracking.

Sharing and third parties

Rocky does not include advertising, analytics, social-login, or third-party data-collection SDKs. Player information is not sold or shared.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until the player chooses Reset progress in Settings or deletes Rocky. Reset progress deletes level completion, stars, and scores. Deleting the app removes Rocky's remaining local data.

Children

Rocky does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other player.

Changes and contact

If Rocky's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the change ships. Questions can be sent through the support contact on Rocky's App Store product page.
